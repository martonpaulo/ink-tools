Tools for your Supernote tablet

Plugins and a tablet app that sync your calendars and to-dos, look up words, edit pictures and send pages to your phone, plus tools for the Mac. Free and open source. Unofficial: not made by Ratta.

Download on GitHub

The tools

  • Plugin

    Account Sync

    Syncs the tablet's To-Do with Todoist, and its Calendar with Google, iCloud and ICS calendars. Every account is off until you connect it.

  • Plugin

    Lasso Actions

    Lasso a handwritten word to insert a picture of it, a short definition, a Wikipedia summary or a translation.

  • Plugin

    Device Transfer

    Sends a page, a selection or typed text to an iPhone or a Mac over LocalSend, and receives text or a picture into the open note.

  • Plugin

    Image Editing

    Makes a lassoed picture readable on e-ink, crops it, erases parts, removes the background or straightens a photographed page.

  • Plugin

    Smart Pen

    Turns a held stroke into a clean shape, and each handwritten word into typed text right after you write it.

  • Tablet app

    Handwriting Language

    Switches the handwriting recognition language of the whole tablet with one tap.

  • Mac app

    Ink Notebook Editor

    Opens a Supernote .note notebook on the Mac, or copies one from the tablet over the cable.

  • Mac command line

    inkctl

    Controls the tablet from the Mac over Wi-Fi or USB: installs plugins, opens notes and runs tests, without root.

Before you install

Ink Tools is unofficial. It is not made, affiliated with or endorsed by Ratta. «Supernote» is a trademark of its owner and names the device only.

Some tools use parts of the tablet that Ratta does not document. A firmware update can change those parts and break a tool until a fix ships. A firmware watch, now being built, will check every such part after an update and report what broke.
