**Ink Tools is unofficial.** It is not made, affiliated with, endorsed or reviewed by Ratta. «Supernote» is a trademark of its owner and names the device only.

Some tools use parts of the tablet's software that Ratta does not document. A firmware update can change those parts and break a feature until a fix ships. The [firmware watch](https://github.com/martonpaulo/ink-tools/issues/293), being built now, checks those parts after an update and reports what broke.

No tool needs root or modified firmware. Use these tools at your own risk, and keep a backup of your notes before you install or update one.
